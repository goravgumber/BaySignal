# Brand Decision — provisional only

**Decision for design work:** keep **BaySignal** as the editable working label only. It is not approved for product rename, public launch or legal use. Repository/package/service/environment/database names and reservation prefixes remain unchanged.

## Rationale and caveat

“Bay” points to the physical unit an operator must inspect; “Signal” suggests an observation requiring provenance rather than an unquestionable truth. Together the phrase fits the parking-state integrity hypothesis better than broad “AI parking” claims. It is descriptive enough to explain the concept but may also be difficult to protect. The name has not been cleared.

A web search on 15 October 2026 surfaced **Baysignal LLC**, an active California entity reported as formed in 2024 ([third-party registry aggregation](https://www.bizprofile.net/ca/redwood-city/baysignal-llc)). This does not establish a parking-sector conflict or trademark right, but it is a real-name collision requiring investigation. Search results are not a trademark/domain clearance.
